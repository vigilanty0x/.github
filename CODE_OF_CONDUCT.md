# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's sensitive information are
not accepted.

Keep technical disagreement focused on observable behavior and reproducible
evidence. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate
these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns through a non-public contact path provided by the
maintainer. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in an issue.
